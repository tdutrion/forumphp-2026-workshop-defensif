# ScreenRoute

ScreenRoute plans a film marathon: pick a city and a day, and it chains the
showtimes of its cinemas into programmes you can actually make, travel time
included. It is free software (GNU AGPL-3.0), and the playground of a
defensive PHP workshop.

- [Self-hosting](self-hosting.md): run your own instance, with Docker, on a
  virtual machine, or on shared hosting (Infomaniak, OVHcloud).
- [Design system](design-system.md): the tokens and Twig components of the
  interface.
- [Trainer guide](exercises.md): the exercises of the workshop.

The source code, and how to run it on your machine, are on
[GitHub](https://github.com/tdutrion/forumphp-2026-workshop-defensif).
